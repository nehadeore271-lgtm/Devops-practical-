# Product Vision Document

## Product Vision Statement
"Develop a secure and user-friendly Student Record and Academic Management Platform that enables educational institutions to efficiently manage student records, attendance, academic performance, course registration, and reporting through a scalable web-based application.[span_9](start_span)"[span_9](end_span)

## Core Objectives
* Streamline academic administrative processes into a single unified platform[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* Provide role-based access for students, faculty, and administrative staff[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* Deliver real-time tracking for attendance, marks, and academic performance[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

## Key Stakeholders & Target Users
* **Students**: View profiles, course enrollments, attendance records, and academic results[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
* **Faculty Members**: Manage course assignments, record daily attendance, enter and update student marks[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
* **Administrators**: Register students, manage courses, configure role-based security, and generate institution-wide reports[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

## Measurable Success Criteria
* 99.9% uptime for core authentication and academic modules[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
